01 / About

Background & Expertise

I am a U.S. Army NCO finishing four years of active duty. I lead a section of 10 to 15 soldiers, serve as the COMSEC handler and primary hand-receipt holder for assigned equipment, and operate a fire direction center under a zero-error standard. The through-line in all of it is the same: keep the records right, follow the written regulation, and escalate the moment something falls outside parameters.

My education runs on two tracks that are more connected than they look. The BSBA and MBA cover budgeting, cost analysis, and how programs are actually funded and run. The M.S. in Cybersecurity Technology covers the security side, from risk frameworks like NIST and RMF down to hands-on host hardening and log analysis. Defense programs need people who can speak both.

Outside of coursework I build small defensive tools and write up what I learn. Everything on my GitHub is read-only and defensive by design: auditors and checkers meant to surface findings for a human to review, not to change anything on a system.

I am open to cleared roles in cybersecurity, IT, and defense program support, and I am willing to relocate anywhere in the U.S.
